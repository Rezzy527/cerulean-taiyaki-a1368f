FAQ

Are your jerseys real?

Yes. Every jersey is an officially licensed product, sourced from the clubs, their kit partners or authorised distributors.

How do I order?

Add jerseys to your bag, fill in your details and send the order request. It opens in WhatsApp with everything filled in, and we reply to confirm stock, delivery cost and payment.

How do the sizes fit?

Fan jerseys fit true to size. If you're between sizes or like a looser fit, go one size up. Ask us on WhatsApp and we'll help you choose.

Do you deliver?

Yes, across South Africa. Delivery cost and timing are confirmed when we review your order.
